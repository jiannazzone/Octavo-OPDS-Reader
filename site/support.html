It helps to know your iPhone model, your iOS version, your Octavo version (Settings > About) and which server you use.

Common problems

My server won't connect over http. Plain http only works with home and VPN addresses, such as 192.168.1.5, nas.local or a Tailscale address. Anything else needs https.

My server's certificate is self-signed. Octavo asks you to trust it when you add the server. If the certificate changes, it asks again.

A book won't open. Octavo reads EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and FB2. It can't open PDFs, comics or files with DRM.
