# Settings (step 14)

Step 14 of `docs/PLAN.md`, built 2026-11-02. Settings replaces the stand-in that opened from the gear at the top of the side column: the server and its page (Sign out, Remove server), Library's one switch, Books on iPhone, and About. The design questions were decided with Adam from picture mockups (`docs/design/direction.md`, Settings in the library pass). Screenshots are in `settings/`.

## What it looks like

- **The list** (frame 34, without its Reading progress row): Server (the name over "address · account"), Library (Titles under covers, moved from the stand-in), Reading (Keep screen on, a menu of Off and 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes; Close book when folded, a switch), Storage (Books on iPhone "12 · 48 MB", or "None"), About (Version "0.1 (1)", Source code ↗ opening GitHub in Safari, Licences ›, and "Octavo is free software under the Mozilla Public License 2.0."). The debug sections stay at the bottom in debug builds.
- **Unfolded: the list on the left page, the chosen page on the right** (frame 37). Settings opens with the server's page there; a tap on the left changes the right page without a slide, and the chosen row is tinted. Folded, the list and each page are screens of their own. A fold keeps the page that was chosen on top (Back then shows the list). Back, or a swipe from the edge, leaves Settings in one step when unfolded.
- **The server's page** (frame 35): Address, Account, Reading progress, then Sign out and Remove server. Reading progress says Synced; Not synced yet with "N books pending." under it; or Paused with "Sign in again to sync." when signed out, where Sign out becomes Sign in (the Sign in again screen). An OPDS catalog has no Reading progress row, as a book's place line shows nothing for it, and no Sign out unless it has a password.
- **Sign out** asks first ("Sign out of BookOrbit?" / "Your downloaded books stay on this iPhone. Reading progress syncs again once you sign in." · Sign out · Cancel; a catalog's message has only the first sentence). The server stays; the tabs show the signed-out banner, reworded for both causes: "Signed out of BookOrbit. Reading still works; sync is paused." The Sign in again screen starts the same way.
- **Remove server** (frame 36): "Remove BookOrbit?" / "Its 12 downloaded books use 48 MB. Remove them too, or keep them on this iPhone?" · Keep books on iPhone (the default) · Remove books too · Cancel. Without downloads: "You can add it again any time." · Remove · Cancel. Then setup, as on a new install.
- **Books on iPhone**: "5 books · 4.4 MB" over the books (cover, title, author, size), with a sort menu at the header's right (Size, Title, Author by last name, Recently downloaded; remembered, Size first). A swipe removes one, keeping its place, as the details' Remove download does; Remove all downloads asks first ("Remove all downloads?" / "5 books · 4.4 MB. Your places in them are kept."). Empty: "No books on iPhone" / "Books you download show here." Rows open nothing.
- **Licences**: Octavo's line, then each component from `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` (bundled with the app) with its licence and full text, the 80-column lines joined back into paragraphs.

## How it works

- **Routes.** `.settings` and `.settingsPage(SettingsPage)` (`server`, `storage`, `licences`). `SettingsScreen` lays either out: folded, the list or the page; unfolded, both, each keeping its place in the view across a fold so a half-typed name survives it. A page names the list as its `ShellRoute.leftPage`, and the shell's navigation treats any such pair the same way, going by the spread the tab's own stack measured (`ShellModel.isTwoPages(in:)`; 2026-11-07, `ui-audit.md` items 1 and 3: a tab out of sight measures one page, so one reading for every tab went wrong after switching tabs): `ShellPath.open` pushes folded and, unfolded, puts the page beside the list at once in place of the one there; `ShellPath.back` takes the page and the list together unfolded, for the Back button and the edge swipe alike; `ScreenStack` hides a screen the one above draws on its left page (`ShellPath.isDrawnByTheOneAbove`), by the same posture Back goes by, so a swipe uncovers the tab. Settings' rows just push their page. Every screen now fills the spread, so `fillsSpread` is gone.
- **Titles.** Settings draws a title on each page with `ScreenTitle`, the view `ScreenFrame` now uses for every screen's title.
- **The server's details** come from `LibraryServer` (`address`, `username`, `signsIn`; `detail` is made from them). Sign in again updates the account shown.
- **Sync status.** `BookPlaces.pendingCount(on:)`: the books on that server in `UnsentPlaces` whose saved place still needs a push.
- **Close book when folded** (`ReaderSettings.closeBookWhenFoldedKey`, on by default). `ShellModel.hingeChanged(.fold)` closes the book only with it on; off, the reader follows the fold as it does unfolding (`ReaderModel.hingeChanged`, `FoldBatch`). A cover still opening the book goes either way. The tour goes through `TourController.hingeChanged` first, so it never reads the setting.
- **Keep screen on** (`ReaderScreenOn`, decision 24). With a book open, `ReaderKeepAwake` sets `isIdleTimerDisabled` until the setting's time after the place last moved (`ReaderModel.moveCount`; a repeat of the same page doesn't count, and a fold counts only if it moves the page's start). Coming back from the background or changing the setting starts the time again. A moment away that isn't the background (Control Center) lets go and carries on with the time left; if the time ran out meanwhile, the screen stays under Auto-Lock. Checked on the simulator with each minute counted as 2 s (2026-11-06). There, Notification Center passed through the background, so closing it started the time again. Not checked: the real 10 minutes, and how soon a real Duo dims once the time is up. **Redraws:** the timer adds none to `ReaderScreen`. It's 2 per animated turn with or without it, because `ReaderView` already follows `relocationCount` (20 turns each, folded, on the simulator at 60 Hz, so not at the Duo's 120).
- **Sign out** (`ServerActions.signOut`): shows the banner at once, then BookOrbit's `BookOrbitClient.signOutEverywhere()` forgets the tokens (memory and Keychain, dropping a refresh in flight) and then sends `POST auth/logout {refreshToken}` (the refresh cookie before 3.0) through the server's `ServerTransport`, without waiting for it (15 s timeout; a failure leaves the session to lapse on the server). Measured on v3.6.0: that revokes the session, so the refresh and access tokens both get 401; a bearer-only logout with no body revokes nothing. An OPDS catalog's `OPDSSource.signOut()` deletes the password from the Keychain and from the client's in-memory cache; a load still reading the store can't put it back (`CredentialCache.generation`).
- **Since decision 36** Remove server takes one server of several, and a removed server's books wait under its own ID rather than being deleted when another is added (`docs/notes/more-than-one-server.md`). The two points below are how it worked with one server.
- **Remove server** (`ServerActions.remove(_:removingBooks:)`), in order: the sign-out above; `ServerCredentials.removeAll(serverID:)` (tokens, password, certificate pin, catalog secret, each tried even if another fails); every download still running is cancelled and leaves no file; the downloads (`serverDownloads`, every book that wasn't imported, since v1 has one server and a file is kept by its book's ID alone, so a book kept from a server removed before shows as downloaded on the one added since). The server's Books on iPhone records go, except the kept books' (some known only from Continue reading, so they're recorded), which Storage keeps listing; with "Remove books too" the files and all its records go. Then the records that can't open without the server (Continue reading, finished marks), its unsent places (`BookPlaces.forget(server:)`), `RemovedServer.json`, `Server.json`, and `LibraryModel.disconnect()`, which shows setup.
- **The next server** (`ServerActions.keep`, which takes back a removed server's books once the new one is saved): `RemovedServer.json` holds the removed server's identity (`LibraryServer.identity`: its kind, its full saved address with scheme, port and path, and the account; two servers behind one proxy differ by path). A catalog whose address carries a key (Kavita) never counts as the same server again, since two accounts differ only by their keys and nothing derived from a key is kept outside the Keychain. BookOrbit numbers files from 1 on every server, so another server's book 17 would otherwise find the removed one's file, place and bookmarks, and send that place and those bookmarks to the wrong server. The same server and account coming back (at a new ID) finds its books, places and bookmarks; any other server, or another account, finds none: the removed server's downloads, places, bookmarks and records are deleted then. Imported books are never touched.
- **A server's late answers are dropped.** `LibraryModel` ignores roots, Home lists and errors that arrive after another connect or disconnect (`connection`), so the old server's failure (signed out, after Remove server) can't show its banner on the new one. Found when a debug connect followed a saved server whose tokens a test run had wiped.

## The slide in (2026-11-04)

Adam saw the left column grow or bounce from the edge, very briefly, as Settings opened unfolded (on his Duo, 2026-11-03). Measured frame by frame in a recording of each display (`simctl io … recordVideo`), reading the edges of a row, on the simulator:

- **Before:** the list's frame started at the screen's edge, and its rows filled it (455 pt wide unfolded) until its left edge came within 20 pt of where it stops. From there the rows' left edge stayed at x = 20 while their right edge, the title and the right page carried on, so the rows and their text shrank by 20 pt over the last 90 ms or so. Folded did the same, by the same 20 pt.
- **Why:** against the screen's left edge a `List`'s rows take a 20 pt margin they don't have anywhere else, and `ScreenStack`'s slide carries the list across that edge. The right page, away from the edge, always moved as one piece. A probe of the list's `UICollectionView` read 0 for its left safe area and layout margin (and the window's left safe area is 0), so where UIKit gets the 20 pt is still unknown. The cells do take it: x = 0 and 455.7 pt wide, then x = 20 and 435.7 pt wide once the list is at the edge.
- **Ruled out:** hiding the list's position from UIKit in the slide. With `.visualEffect { $0.offset(x:) }` the rows shrank the same way. With a `GeometryEffect` translation the probe still read the list moving, and the slide dropped to 3 frames in 0.4 s (with the probe running). A `UIHostingController` per screen is what's left; not tried, since it's most of a `NavigationStack` rebuilt.
- **Now:** `SettingsPageList` states the margin with `contentMargins(.leading, …, for: .scrollContent)`: `SpreadMetrics.margin` at the screen's edge (Settings' list, and a page folded), 0 on the right page unfolded. A stated margin replaces the system's rather than adding to it, whichever is larger (0 put folded rows at the screen's edge), so it doesn't depend on the system's number. The list's frame still reaches the screen's edge. No other `List` joins the slide: the reader's panels don't use `ScreenStack`, and `TrustServerView` is only reached from the debug section.
- **Measured** (frame 12): rows keep their width in every frame, opening unfolded and folded, a page opening folded, and Back and a slow swipe unfolded. At rest they sit where they did: unfolded 14 of 5.1M pixels differ (video noise), folded 334 of 2.4M with every row's edges in the same place (one pixel differs by more than 32 levels).

## Security review (2026-11-02)

An independent review of the Sign out and Remove server path (tokens, Keychain, the logout request, removal order, debug code). Nothing High. **Adam signed it off on 2026-11-02**, with the fixes below.

| # | Severity | Finding | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Kept books (the default) were taken for another BookOrbit server's books of the same file number: shown as downloaded, opened at the old place, the old server's unsent place and bookmarks sent to the new one | Fixed: `RemovedServer` (above). The full fix, a server in the storage key, waits for a second server |
| 2 | Medium | Remove books too missed downloads still running, which then landed (and could open the reader over setup); a cancel just after the file landed left it | Fixed: `BookOpener.cancelAll`, the shell's pending opens cleared, a file that lands after a cancel is deleted |
